# R64.2 fees and Partner matrix

## Platform fee
- Club: 1.5%
- Premium: 1.5%
- Enterprise: 0%
- Brand Start: 1.5%
- Brand Growth: 1.5%
- Brand Enterprise: 0%
- Federation/eligible Professional punctual transactions: configurable; initial commercial rule 1.5% when applicable.

Ticketing buyer management fee (1.50 €/ticket) is a separate concept from organization platform fee.

## Partner
- 1–9 active paid referred Clubs: 25%.
- 10+ active paid referred Clubs: 30%.
- Federation with specific agreement: 30%.
- Commission base: taxable subscription base excluding VAT/extras.
- Monthly billing: commissions on installments 2–13 only.
- 5 active paid referred Clubs: 100% Federation subscription bonification while requirements remain met.
- Referred Club annual-billing payout rule: **pending commercial decision; do not invent/auto-pay**.
